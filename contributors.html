Recognition for policy contributors in the XPolicyLab & RoboTwin ecosystem

XPolicyLab is an open-source framework that standardizes the full robot policy lifecycle — data formatting, training, deployment, and post-training reinforcement — with support for remote inference and parallel multi-environment rollout. We are building toward a unified specification adopted across research and industry, with planned integrations for starVLA, RLinf, and other model infrastructure.

If you contributed a policy to RoboTwin 3.0 through XPolicyLab (released under an alternate name at launch), thank you. We would like to formally recognize your work and invite you to stay involved as the ecosystem grows.

Contributor Benefits

  1. 1

    Live Leaderboard Placement

    Your policy will be listed on the latest RoboTwin 3.0 and RoboTwin 2.0 leaderboards. Researchers can one-click export Leaderboard TeX, giving your work lasting visibility within a unified, continuously updated ranking system.

  2. 2

    Official Acknowledgment

    You will be credited as a Community Contributor in the RoboTwin 3.0 paper appendix, formally recognizing your model contribution to the benchmark.

  3. 3

    XPolicyLab Co-Authorship

    We invite eligible contributors to join as co-authors on the XPolicyLab paper and help shape the standard as it evolves.

  4. 4

    Research Collaboration

    Interested in the synergy between model development and evaluation? We welcome your ideas and may invite you to co-author related survey topics, with planned submission to IEEE TPAMI.

XPolicyLab 是面向机器人策略模型的开源标准框架，统一规范数据处理、训练、部署与强化后训练的全链路接口， 并支持远程推理与多环境并行部署。我们正逐步接入 starVLA、RLinf 等模型基础设施， 目标成为研究界与工业界广泛采用的统一规范生态。

若您已通过 XPolicyLab 向 RoboTwin 3.0（发布时另有用名）贡献过策略模型，谨此致谢。 我们希望正式认可您的工作，并邀请您持续参与生态共建。

贡献者权益

  1. 1

    榜单持续更新

    您贡献的策略将同步更新至 RoboTwin 3.0 与 RoboTwin 2.0 最新榜单。 用户可一键复制 Leaderboard TeX 表格，在统一榜单体系中持续扩大模型影响力。

  2. 2

    正式致谢

    您将被列入 RoboTwin 3.0 论文附录的 Community Contributor， 作为对模型贡献的正式认可。

  3. 3

    XPolicyLab 共同作者

    我们诚挚邀请符合条件的贡献者成为 XPolicyLab 论文的共同作者， 与我们一起维护并推动模型标准生态的发展。

  4. 4

    科研合作机会

    若您对模型开发与评测的双向协同感兴趣，欢迎分享您的想法。 我们非常乐意邀请您参与相关 Survey 主题的撰写，计划投稿至 IEEE TPAMI。
